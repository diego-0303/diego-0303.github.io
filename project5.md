  <h2 style="font-size: 2rem; margin-bottom: 8px;">ERNIE: Mars Rover Project</h2>
  <h3 style="font-size: 1.1rem; color: #666; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 30px;">Caltech CRATER Team Project</h3>
  
  <p>
    This rover project, dubbed ERNIE, was my first college experience participating in an engineering project team. As part of the Caltech Rover, Autonomy, Technology and Research (CRATER) team, I contributed to the mechanical design, stress analysis, and fabrication of critical rover components.
  </p>

  <figure style="margin: 35px 0; text-align: center;">
    <img src="https://diego-0303.github.io/images/ERNIE.png" alt="ERNIE Rover Early Design Iteration" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08);">
    <figcaption style="margin-top: 10px; font-size: 0.9rem; color: #666; font-style: italic;">Figure 1: Early CAD design and chassis layout for ERNIE Version 1.</figcaption>
  </figure>

  <p>
    My role focused on several key mechanical subsystems, including the robotic arm and suspension system. One of the most exciting subsystems was the rocker-bogie suspension system, adapted from NASA JPL rover heritage. This passive link mechanism allows the rover to maintain equal wheel contact and stability over rough obstacle-laden terrain.
  </p>

  <figure style="margin: 35px 0; text-align: center;">
    <img src="https://diego-0303.github.io/images/ERNIE2.jpeg" alt="ERNIE Rover Assembled Hardware" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08);">
    <figcaption style="margin-top: 10px; font-size: 0.9rem; color: #666; font-style: italic;">Figure 2: Fully assembled ERNIE rover featuring the rocker-bogie suspension system during mobility testing.</figcaption>
  </figure>

  <p>
    I also led much of the mechanical design for the 6-DOF robotic arm, creating custom high-torque joint assemblies capable of satisfying payload requirements for equipment servicing and science sample collection tasks.
  </p>

  <figure style="margin: 35px 0; text-align: center;">
    <img src="https://diego-0303.github.io/images/ARM_JOINT.jpg" alt="Robotic Arm Custom Joint Fabrication" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08);">
    <figcaption style="margin-top: 10px; font-size: 0.9rem; color: #666; font-style: italic;">Figure 3: Custom machined robotic arm joint designed for high torque transmission.</figcaption>
  </figure>

  <p>
    The rover was designed to satisfy the rigorous requirements of the Canadian International Rover Challenge (CIRC) and the University Rover Challenge (URC). These competitions test autonomous navigation, science sampling, equipment servicing, and extreme terrain traversal in simulated Martian environments.
  </p>
