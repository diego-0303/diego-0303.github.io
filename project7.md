  <h2 style="font-size: 2rem; margin-bottom: 8px;">Structural Health Monitoring for Earthquake Engineering</h2>
  <h3 style="font-size: 1.1rem; color: #666; font-weight: normal; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 30px;">Caltech Earthquake Engineering Lab — Prof. Monica Kohler</h3>
  
  <p>
    Over the summer of my freshman year at Caltech, I worked under Professor Monica Kohler in her earthquake engineering lab. This research focused on developing algorithms to detect real-time changes in structural integrity for high-rise buildings subjected to seismic events.
  </p>

  <figure style="margin: 35px 0; text-align: center;">
    <img src="https://diego-0303.github.io/images/EARTHQuAKE.jpg" alt="Seismic Acceleration Data & Structural Array" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08);">
    <figcaption style="margin-top: 10px; font-size: 0.9rem; color: #666; font-style: italic;">Figure 1: Spectral acceleration response analysis from multi-channel building accelerometer sensor networks.</figcaption>
  </figure>

  <p>
    We analyzed live accelerometer data recorded across a 10-story building on campus alongside historical earthquake motion datasets. A primary focus was evaluating CPU vs. GPU parallelization pipelines to accelerate real-time spectral matrix computations, enabling emergency structural damage alerts.
  </p>
